\subsection{Transport of the actual polarization, torsion and level}
\label{subsec:ftb-relative-polarization-level}

The algebraic laws used here are the actual clauses of
\(\mathrm{Out.Laws}\): multiplication by a positive integer
is finite and is etale when that integer is a unit;
the Weil commutator relation exists locally, is unique,
bilinear and compatible with pullback; and the stated
polarized base changes preserve the actual line bundle.
The fixed-fibre \(\mathrm{ThetaUnifAV}\) used below is
the one constructed in
Proposition~\ref{prop:ftb-full-fixed-fibre-theta-realization}.
No family theta frame is assumed in this subsection.

\begin{lemma}[Constancy of actual torsion and Weil relations]
\label{lem:ftb-holomorphic-torsion-weil-constant}
Let \(X\) be locally Noetherian, and let \(P/X\) be the
given principally polarized abelian scheme with line \(L\).
Let \(B\subset\mathbb C^m\) be open and connected, with
holomorphic base points \(y(b)\).
Let \(x_b,x'_b\in P_{y(b)}(\mathbb C)\) be
regular-evaluation-holomorphic and killed by \(k>0\),
where \(k\) is a unit on \(X\).
Equality \(x_{b_0}=x'_{b_0}\) implies equality for every \(b\).
If their actual Weil relation at \(b_0\) has scalar
\(c\in\mathbb C^\times\), then the actual Weil relation
has that same scalar at every \(b\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Form the actual \(k\)-torsion scheme
\[
             N=P\times_{[k],\,P,\,1}X,\qquad q:N\to X.
\]
It is finite etale, by the multiplication law and
invertibility of \(k\).
The maps \(b\mapsto x_b,x'_b\) lift to holomorphic maps
\(a,a':B\to N(\mathbb C)\).
Indeed, on affine fibre-product charts the coordinate
ring is generated by the two factor rings, so the
evaluations of these lifts are holomorphic.
The pair gives a holomorphic map
\(z:B\to(N\times_XN)(\mathbb C)\).
The diagonal of \(q\) is open, since \(q\) is unramified,
and closed, since \(q\) is separated.
Its inverse image under \(z\) is therefore clopen in \(B\).
It contains \(b_0\) if the two torsion points agree there,
and hence is all of \(B\).
This proves the equality assertion on actual scheme points.

For the Weil assertion use the two tautological torsion
sections on \(T=N\times_XN\).
The Weil law gives a unit \(\epsilon\in\Gamma(T,\mathcal O_T)^\times\)
expressing their commutator relation.
Bilinearity and uniqueness give \(\epsilon^k=1\):
bilinearity computes the pairing of \(x^k=1\) with the
second point as \(\epsilon^k\); the pairing with the
identity is \(1\), since its scalar equals its own square.
Pull the relation to the complex point \(z(b)\).
Its scalar is the evaluation \(\epsilon(z(b))\), by
actual pullback of generators.
This scalar varies continuously and belongs to the
finite set of \(k\)-th roots of unity.
Connectedness makes it constant.
At \(b_0\) uniqueness of the Weil scalar identifies it
with \(c\), proving the assertion at every \(b\).
This argument uses a unit on the actual torsion-pair
scheme, not the existence of arbitrary continuous
choices of theta-group lifts.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The actual Weil relation under a polarized base change]
\label{lem:ftb-weil-actual-polarized-pullback}
Suppose \(P'/S'\to P/S\) is an actual group pullback,
and the supplied line isomorphism identifies \(L'\)
with the pullback of \(L\).
For a pair of commuting points over the same test
scheme, identified by that pullback, the \(\mathrm{WeilIs}\)
relation with a specified scalar is equivalent on the
two sides.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Use the line isomorphism to transport a theta-group
generator, together with its inverse transport on the
group pullback.
In local line coordinates the transport factor for
the lift over a point \(x\) is a quotient
\[
                             u(p)^k/u(p+x)^k,
\]
where \(u\) is the corresponding evaluated invertible
line-comparison coefficient.
In the two compositions over \(x,y\), the factors become
\[
       \frac{u(p)^k}{u(p+x)^k}
       \frac{u(p+x)^k}{u(p+x+y)^k}
       \quad\text{and}\quad
       \frac{u(p)^k}{u(p+y)^k}
       \frac{u(p+y)^k}{u(p+y+x)^k}.
\]
They agree by commutativity.
Cancel these common units to obtain exactly the same
commutator scalar.
This calculation is made after every test morphism and
on every common line chart; it is an identity of the
generator coefficients themselves and applies also to
nonreduced test schemes.
The group-pullback isomorphism over the identical test
scheme gives inverse transport, proving equivalence.
Finally the local existence and universal-generator
clauses in \(\mathrm{WeilIs}\) transfer in both directions
by these two transports.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[The positive Hermitian form with the prescribed integer values]
\label{prop:ftb-positive-form-from-actual-weil}
Let \(e:\mathbb C^g\to P(\mathbb C)\) be an actual
exponential with both tangent clauses, a discrete kernel
and the local lifting property proved above.
Let \(L_0\) be an abelian group with integer bilinear
form \(E_F\), and let \(Q:L_0\to\ker e\) be additive.
Suppose that for every \(k>0\) and \(a,b\in L_0\) the
actual Weil relation at
\(e(Qa/k),e(Qb/k)\) has scalar
\[
                         \exp(-2\pi i E_F(a,b)/k).          \tag{PW1}
\]
Then there is a positive definite Hermitian form \(H\)
on \(\mathbb C^g\) such that
\[
                         \operatorname{Im}H(Qa,Qb)=E_F(a,b).
                                                               \tag{PW2}
\]
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Use the actual polarized base change to the complex
fibre and the fixed-fibre construction to obtain
\(u_*=(e_*,\Lambda_*,f_*)\), positive Hermitian \(H_*\),
and integral \(E_*=\operatorname{Im}H_*|_{\Lambda_*}\).
Lift \(e_*\) locally through \(e\) by the supplied local
lifting property.
The derivative of this lift gives a complex linear
\(F\) with \(eF=e_*\): subtract the value at \(0\),
use discreteness to make the additive defect vanish
near \(0\), and extend the additive germ as in (PK5).

The source proves this \(F\) is an isomorphism directly
from the two discrete kernels and the two surjections.
A discrete subgroup of a finite-dimensional real vector
space is countable.
Thus \(\ker F\), lying in \(\ker e_*\), is countable;
a nonzero real vector subspace is uncountable, so
\(\ker F=0\).
For every \(v\) in the target, surjectivity of \(e_*\)
gives a \(w\) with \(v-Fw\in\ker e\).
The real vector space \(\operatorname{coker}F\) is
therefore a quotient of a countable set.
It too must be zero.
The inverse is continuous in finite dimensions.
In particular \(F^{-1}Qa\in\Lambda_*\).

The fixed-fibre formula gives the Weil scalar
\(\exp(-2\pi i E_*(F^{-1}Qa,F^{-1}Qb)/k)\).
Lemma~\ref{lem:ftb-weil-actual-polarized-pullback}
transports that \emph{actual} relation to \(P\).
Uniqueness at the complex field point compares it
with (PW1).
The two integer exponents differ by a multiple of \(k\)
for every positive \(k\).
Choosing \(k\) larger than the absolute value of their
difference makes that difference zero.
Thus
\[
                 E_*(F^{-1}Qa,F^{-1}Qb)=E_F(a,b).
\]
Set \(H(v,w)=H_*(F^{-1}v,F^{-1}w)\).
Complex linearity and injectivity of \(F^{-1}\) preserve
positive definiteness, and the preceding equality proves
(PW2).
No integer polarization form is recovered from a single
torsion order.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[The exact local PEL period lift]
\label{prop:ftb-actual-local-pel-period-lift}
Use the actual source packet
\[
        E,\ H,\ D,\ l,\ X_o,\ \sigma,\ g,\ \mathrm{ty},
                    \quad D',\ t:D.L\to D'.L .
\]
Here \(E\) is a CM number field, \(H\) is its host,
\(D,D'\) are the given Hermitian lattice data,
\(l=(M,\mathfrak M,A,\ldots)\) is the given level,
\(X_o\) is its \emph{supplied} fine moduli object with
actual universal abelian scheme, coefficient action,
polarizing line and sections \(\eta,\eta_A\);
\(X_o\) is smooth over \(K_A(l)\).
Assume \(1\le D.p+D.q\),
\(\sigma(\rho)=H.\mathrm{rt}_0\), and
\(\mathrm{IsTypeFn}(H,D.p,D.q,\mathrm{ty})\);
thus
\[
                         g=\tfrac12[E:\mathbb Q](D.p+D.q).
\]
The map \(t\) is the given \(\mathcal O_E\)-linear map
appearing in the markings.

Let \(B\subset\mathbb C^m\) be open with \(b_0\in B\),
and let \(y:B\to X_o(\mathbb C)\) be
regular-evaluation-holomorphic over the fixed
\(\sigma:K_A(l)\hookrightarrow\mathbb C\).
Prescribe an actual central exponential \(e_0\)
and a central \(\mathrm{IsPeriod}(D',\mathrm{ty},P_0)\).
Suppose their full kernel, coefficient action and
all-order Weil relations agree:
\[
\begin{gathered}
 \ker e_0=P_0(D'.L),\qquad
 e_0(\mathrm{ty}(a)v)=[a]e_0(v),\\
 e_k\bigl(e_0(P_0x/k),e_0(P_0x'/k)\bigr)
                   =\exp(-2\pi iD'.E_F(x,x')/k)
                         \quad(k>0),                       \tag{PW3}
\end{gathered}
\]
where the second line denotes the actual \(\mathrm{WeilIs}\)
relation with the displayed scalar, and
\(\mathrm{ty}(a)v=(\mathrm{ty}_i(a)v_i)_i\).
Assume the exact central marking identities
\[
 \eta_{y(b_0)}(a)=e_0(M^{-1}P_0(ta))
       \quad(a\in\overline{\mathfrak M}\,D.L),\qquad
 \eta_{A,y(b_0)}(a)=e_0(A^{-1}P_0(ta))\quad(a\in D.L).
                                                               \tag{PW4}
\]
Then, on an open \(b_0\in B'\subset B\), there are
\(P_b,e_b\) with central values \(P_0,e_0\), such that
\(P\) is \(\mathrm{IsHolPeriods}(D',\mathrm{ty},B',P)\),
\(e\) is jointly regular-evaluation-holomorphic, and
all clauses of (PW3)--(PW4) hold for every \(b\in B'\)
with \(P_0,e_0\) replaced by \(P_b,e_b\).
Every \(e_b\) has the full exponential and tangent clauses.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The actual universal object supplies an abelian scheme
of the displayed relative dimension and a rigidified
symmetric principal line.
Its base changes retain these data.
Apply Proposition~\ref{prop:ftb-actual-relative-exponential},
then the full-kernel and coefficient-action construction
of Subsection~\ref{subsec:ftb-relative-period-kernel-action}.
Before its final conjugation, write the maps as
\(\widehat e_b,\widehat P_b\).
Restrict to one connected parameter ball on which all
those conclusions hold.

For every fixed positive \(k\), the actual points
\[
                       x_b=\widehat e_b(\widehat P_bx/k)
\]
are holomorphic and \(k\)-torsion, since
\[
 x_b^k=\widehat e_b(\widehat P_bx)=1.
\]
The integer \(k\) is a unit on the characteristic-zero
moduli base.
Lemma~\ref{lem:ftb-holomorphic-torsion-weil-constant}
therefore transports (PW3) from the central fibre for
every \(x,x',k\).
It uses the same connected ball for each relation;
there is no need to intersect infinitely many newly
shrunk balls.

The actual sections \(\eta(a)\) are \(M\)-torsion.
In the source this follows from the exact kernel
condition for \(\eta\) and the inclusion
\(M\in\mathfrak M\):
multiplication of any \(a\in D.L\) by \(M\) lies in
\(\mathfrak M D.L\), where \(\eta\) is zero.
Similarly the defining kernel condition for \(\eta_A\)
makes it \(A\)-torsion.
These algebraic sections and
\(\widehat e_b(\widehat P_b(ta)/M)\), respectively
\(\widehat e_b(\widehat P_b(ta)/A)\),
are actual holomorphic torsion families.
The equality part of the same lemma transports (PW4)
on that ball.
The domain restriction
\(a\in\overline{\mathfrak M}D.L\) on the first marking
is retained.

For each \(b\), apply
Proposition~\ref{prop:ftb-positive-form-from-actual-weil}
to the transported all-order relations.
It gives a positive Hermitian form with imaginary
values \(D'.E_F\) on \(\widehat P_b(D'.L)\).
Use now exactly the final holomorphic conjugation \(h_b\)
from (PK9):
\[
                   P_b=h_b^{-1}\widehat P_b,\qquad
                         e_b(v)=\widehat e_b(h_bv).
\]
It makes \(P_b\) \(\mathcal O_E\)-linear for the fixed
type and keeps both central values unchanged.
The positive form pulls back by the invertible complex
linear \(h_b\), so its imaginary values on \(P_b\)
remain \(D'.E_F\).
The remaining clauses of \(\mathrm{IsPeriod}\) are precisely
injectivity, discreteness and full real span already
proved for this same kernel.
Every \(P_bx\) is holomorphic, giving
\(\mathrm{IsHolPeriods}\).
Finally \(h_bP_bx=\widehat P_bx\), including after division
by \(k,M,A\).
Thus all transported Weil and marking identities persist
literally after the conjugation.
The full exponential clauses persist by the complex
linear chain rule.
\end{proof}
