# Ordinary RH through fourth-power samples

This is a working exposition, **not a completed standalone unconditional proof of RH**.

The manuscript proves the prime-only analytic criterion and ending, the signed finite-group comparison, conductor-one field and degree bounds, cyclotomic class-number oddness, strict descent at 3 and 7, the full prime-to-degree minus estimate, and trivial rational-character vanishing at the exceptional and resonant primes. It proves the plus-height assembly from two precisely stated rational-character estimates; those two estimates remain assumptions.

The dyadic argument now includes the 24-generator bound, bidual and finite-twist identifications, restriction back to the canonical CM field, uniform free-lift congruences, cokernel growth, matrix-logarithm centers, a fixed exceptional set of at most 24 twists, and an explicit discriminant bound at depth seven. The common-window dyadic estimate is proved **conditional on the specialized high-twist bound K_2(C_K)**. The proof of that cohomology bound remains outstanding. The local calculation is complete: L_m has exactly two dyadic places, and the H² length is s_2(L_m,a)+m+2+v_2(a), whenever finite. Hence H² grows from depth seven by at most 25(m-7)_+.

The spectral comparison includes the centered prime-distribution identity, the global zero-growth bound and a spectral uniqueness lemma. The independent geometric comparisons cover smooth logarithmic integrability, determinant-prescribed fractional-ideal lattices with exact covolume scaling, and positive-definite shifted lattice theta; their stated domains matter. The fractional-ideal proof discharges the SStein comparison in the high-twist class-counting chain.

The new certificate snapshot is pinned at 6186b8d5c272c08c90f6f5cce1a25d22232ed78d. Its Q(sqrt(-3)) refinement is proved in prose here, including class number, conductor, character-order and inertia conditions. Its substantial PropGK producer and nontrivial resonant estimate remain inputs.

The remaining work is the two rational-character estimates (or a valid weaker cubic-logarithmic plus-height bound), the high-twist cohomology estimate K_2, and the construction of the connected geometric families or signed data with the required uniform theta, ramified matching, cusp, metric and integral-lattice estimates. The supporting longer-route chapters retain their explicit construction, automorphy and compatibility hypotheses.

The reviewed 552-line conductor-one reduction, its 630-line revision, and subsequent two-character reduction are useful implications with imported mathematical content. The prose proofs added here have not been separately formalized. Compilation and rendering check the document, not the mathematical completeness of the proof.
